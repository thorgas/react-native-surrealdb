# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project is expected to help create a welcoming,
professional, and harassment-free environment. Be respectful, assume good
faith, discuss technical ideas rather than people, and make room for different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and disclosure boundaries; and
- focusing on what is best for users and the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, trolling, deliberate intimidation, publishing
someone else's private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, use GitHub's report and block tools or open a
brief issue asking the maintainer for a private contact channel. Do not place
sensitive personal information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities
must follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily ban,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation
against anyone making a good-faith report is not permitted.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and
whether harm was acknowledged and repaired.
